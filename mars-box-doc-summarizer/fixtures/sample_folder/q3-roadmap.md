# Q3 Product Roadmap

The Q3 roadmap focuses on onboarding speed and enterprise readiness. We plan to cut time-to-first-value from two days to under one hour by shipping guided setup.

## Guided setup
A five-step wizard replaces the current docs-first flow.

## SSO and audit logs
Enterprise customers asked for SAML SSO and exportable audit logs before renewal.

## Pricing page refresh
Simplify tiers from four to three and show annual savings.
